5CS045 Week 1 Tutorial
======================

SETUP

1. Unzip this folder into your Full Stack folder.
2. Open the folder in Sublime Text.
3. Open index.html in Chrome. It will look broken. That is the start.


WHAT YOU DO

Edit style.css only. Do not edit index.html.

Section A is given. Leave it alone.
Section B and Section C are your work.

Find each blank. It looks like this:

    /* 1a */ justify-content: ________;

Replace ________ with the value your tutor gives you.

Your tutor will also ask you to change some values that already work,
look at the result, and change them back.


AT THE END

Send the folder to the server with scp:

    ~/public_html/week1-tutorial/

Open it here:

    http://student.heraldcollege.edu.np/~yourusername/week1-tutorial/

Then open it on your phone.
