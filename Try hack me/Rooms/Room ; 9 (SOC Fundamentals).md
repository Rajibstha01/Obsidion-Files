A **SOC** (**S**ecurity **O**perations **C**enter) is a dedicated facility operated by a specialized security team. This team aims to continuously monitor an organization’s network and resources and identify suspicious activity to prevent damage.

## Task 2: Purpose and component

The main purpose of the SOC is to keep ==🟢Detection== and ==🟢response== in check.

* ### Detection 
	* Detecting vulnerabilities:
		Having unauthorized access by exploiting a loop hole (vulnerabilities) in a system. 
		
	* Detect unauthorized activities. 
		Unauthorized activities mean other than usual. Like a employee login from another country without any headups. 
		
	* Detect policies violation. 
		Policy are set of rule enforced to make the company walk in right path. Seeing if that policy are uphold and not broken which may make the company at risk of threats. 
		 
	* Detect intrusion.  
		Intrusions refer to unauthorized access to systems and networks. 
* ### Response
	* support with incident: 
		If having secured all of the thing an incident might happen the SOC must support the team and help secure the company by any means .
### The three ==🟢pillars== of SOC are as follow.
* People 
* Process 
* Technology

**People**, **Process**, and **Technology** coexist in a SOC environment. A team of professional individuals working on state-of-the-art security tools in the presence of proper processes is what makes a mature SOC environment.

## Task 3: People 

There are many role in SOC team and due to the current trend in automation most of the things can be automatic but the human intervention is always necessary in any type of security work so the team are as follow:

Team member: 
* SOC manager
	SOC Manager keep check on all of the member of the team knowing what they are doing and if the things are done properly with requirement are meet and also keep in tab with CISO(Chief Information Security Officer). 
	
* SOC Analyst (level 1)
	Anything detected by the system goes through level 1 first. 
	
* SOC Analyst (level 2)
	anything that need deep drive that can't be done by level 1. it also does the research and other things. 
	
* SOC Analyst (level 3)
	anything that need more through check that can't be done by level 2.
	detailed responses, including containment, eradication, and recovery are done by level 3. 
	
* Security Engineer
	all of the solution done by soc analysts are gone through him and he configures them to run the company's system smoothly. 
	 
* Detection Engineer
	 Security rules are the logic built behind security solutions to detect harmful activities. it is often done by level 2 and level 3 analyst but some company make a different role called detection engineer.
