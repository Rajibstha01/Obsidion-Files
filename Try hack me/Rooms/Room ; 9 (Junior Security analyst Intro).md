## Task 1: Junior Security analyst Journey

A world of evolving threats, where every click could hide an attack, and every attack could destroy the business.
In this task it talk about how the threats are more in this modern age and will be taught to see and stop those threats and making sure that the company name of your will not be there in the news. 

The task performed by a SOC level 1: 
- Monitor and investigate various security alerts
- Participate in SOC brainstorms and workshops
- Cooperate with other teams to keep your company safe
- Constantly learn and discover new attacks and defenses
